Doodle Dunes — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 Developer: QG Studio Contact: hien.quachngoc07@gmail.com

Doodle Dunes is a puzzle game for iPhone made by QG Studio. This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and how you can control it.

What the game keeps

Doodle Dunes keeps only two things:

  • your progress — the stars you have earned on each level, your gems, and your daily and weekly challenge results;
  • your settings — sound volume, music volume and haptics.

Both are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so it comes back when you reinstall the game and stays the same on your other iPhones. Your settings are never copied to iCloud. None of this is ever sent to us: we cannot see or access the copy on your iPhone or the one in your iCloud. Without iCloud, everything stays on your iPhone and the game works the same.

You can clear your progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress. That clears your stars, gems and challenge results on this iPhone and in iCloud (your settings and ad-privacy choices stay as they are). Deleting the game deletes the copy on your iPhone; the copy in iCloud stays until you reset your progress in the game.

No account

There is no sign-up, login or account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Ads

Doodle Dunes is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear between levels (not in the first two levels and never while you draw), as a small banner at the bottom of the menu, level-select and play screens, and — only when you choose to watch one — as a short ad that gives you more ink after yours runs out. Gems are never given for watching ads and cannot be bought.

To show and measure ads, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, or an app-specific identifier;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them, and how you use the app (for example app launches and taps);
  • diagnostic and performance information (for example crash logs and launch times).

Google uses this information for advertising and analytics, as described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

QG Studio does not receive this information in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell personal information.

Your choices: consent and tracking

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy. (The row only appears where a consent choice applies.)
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency). The game asks iOS permission before ads may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you decline either, the game works exactly the same; ads may still appear, but they are not personalised.

Children

Doodle Dunes is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will help.
