Doodle Dunes — help

Draw the path, roll the beads, fill the sacks. Every bead has a sack of its own colour waiting on the path below.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag a finger through the sand to draw a path. Beads roll down whatever line you draw, so steer each one into the open sack of the same colour, carried by a worker with a wheelbarrow or a donkey. No line goes through grey rock — draw around it. When a sack is full its carrier walks off, and the next one in that spot's queue (the coloured dots on the curb) walks in. Deliver every bead to win. The first levels show a hand that traces the way.

How are stars earned? The bar at the top is your ink. Use less of it for more stars: finish with plenty left for three stars. A level finished with extra ink (from an ad or gems) earns one star — replay it to earn more.

Why did my level fail? The card tells you which of these happened:

  • Wrong colour sack — a bead dropped into a sack of another colour.
  • That sack was already full — a bead arrived after its sack was full.
  • A bead missed the sacks — a bead reached the path, or a spot with no carrier.
  • Out of ink — the ink ran out before every bead was delivered.

Tap Try again to restart the level. Tip: think before you draw, and let one colour finish before opening the way for the next.

How do levels unlock? In order — beating a level opens the next. You can replay any level you've reached from Levels to improve your stars.

Daily and weekly challenges They open once you've cleared level 7 — tap Challenges on the main menu. There's a new daily puzzle every day at midnight (your local time) and a bigger weekly puzzle every Monday. Win the daily for 1 gem per star and the weekly for 3 gems per star, and replay to beat your stars for more. Win the daily 7 days in a row for 3 bonus gems. Missed days can't be played later.

What are gems? Gems come from the challenges, and you get 3 on your first visit. When your ink runs out, 3 gems give you half a bar more — even offline. A try can be refilled twice: once with an ad and once with gems, or twice with gems. A level finished with extra ink earns one star. Gems can't be bought. If you set your phone's clock back, challenges still play but pay no gems, and the card says so.

How do the ads work? Doodle Dunes is free and paid for by ads. A full-screen ad can appear between levels, when a level has ended and you tap to go on — never while you are drawing, and never in the first two levels. From level 3, if your ink runs out before the beads reach their sacks, the game pauses and offers a short ad — or 3 gems, if you have them — for half a bar of ink. That's up to you: tap No thanks and the level ends as usual. A small banner sits at the bottom of the menu, level-select and play screens, below the board, so it never covers the game. If you are offline, there are simply no ads and the game plays the same. There is no paid "remove ads" option in this version.

How do I change my privacy or consent choice? In the game, Settings → Ad privacy. If you don't see that row, no consent choice is required where you are. Tracking is separate: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I reset my progress? Settings → Reset progress, then confirm. Your stars, gems and challenge results clear, on this iPhone and in iCloud, and only level 1 stays open. Your sound settings and ad-privacy choices are kept. This can't be undone.

I lost my progress. If you're signed in to iCloud, reinstalling the game brings your progress back, usually within a minute of opening it with a connection. Without iCloud, progress lives only on your iPhone, so deleting the app, or moving to a new phone without a backup, loses it. If progress vanished while the app stayed installed, please write in — that's a bug and we want to know.

Sound and haptics Sound volume, music volume and haptics are in Settings.

Does it work offline? Yes. Everything, including the challenges, is played on your iPhone; only the ads and iCloud sync need a connection.

Is my data collected? No account, and nothing is sent to us. The game keeps your stars, gems, challenge results and settings on your phone, and a copy of your progress in your own iCloud if you're signed in. The ads collect some data, explained in the privacy policy.
