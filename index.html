Doodle Dunes

Draw the path, roll the beads, fill the sacks. A relaxing puzzle for iPhone: draw a path through the sand and steer every bead into the sack of its own colour, carried by a worker with a wheelbarrow or a patient donkey.

Features

  • 110 hand-tuned and generated levels that grow from a single bead to busy boards with many colours, queues and rock
  • Earn up to three stars by using less ink
  • Calm desert music and soft, satisfying bead sounds
  • Plays offline, no account
  • English and Vietnamese

Free, supported by ads. Support · Privacy Policy
